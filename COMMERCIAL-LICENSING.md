# Tourmaline Commercial Licensing

Tourmaline Community License 1.0 is free for independent individuals and
organizations whose worldwide **corporate-group annual revenue is below USD
$10 million**. An organization at or above that threshold must have a separate
written Commercial License for new development using covered Tourmaline releases.

This page explains the proposed commercial offering. **It is not itself a
license grant, price quotation, or binding offer.** A Commercial License takes
effect only when the applicable parties execute a separate written agreement.

## Illustrative annual pricing

| Corporate-group annual revenue | Proposed annual organization license |
| --- | ---: |
| Below $10 million | Free under the Community License |
| $10 million to below $50 million | $5,000 USD |
| $50 million through $250 million | $15,000 USD |
| Above $250 million | Negotiated enterprise terms |

These figures are **illustrative launch prices, not guaranteed or validated
market rates**. Quoted terms can reflect scope, support, integrations, and
specific contracting requirements. There is no per-seat, per-project,
per-game, or runtime royalty in the proposed standard organization-wide model.

## Proposed standard contract scope

A written agreement would normally specify the licensed legal entity and
covered corporate affiliates; the Tourmaline versions and updates made
available during its term; authorized employees and contractors; unlimited
internal and customer-facing projects within the covered group; rights to
modify and compile the engine; and royalty-free binary runtime distribution
within End Products. Support, integrations, security reporting, confidentiality,
indemnities, liability, governing law, invoicing, and termination would be
addressed in the signed contract.

## Required continuation language for negotiated agreements

The following language is **recommended operative language to be included in
the signed Commercial License**. It is not automatically incorporated merely
by appearing on this information page:

> **Surviving Distribution Rights.** For every End Product lawfully released
> during the paid license term, Licensee and its lawful successors, publishers,
> and distributors retain a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free right to
> reproduce, distribute, and operate the End Product and the Tourmaline runtime
> and engine modifications incorporated in it. No license renewal, per-copy
> charge, royalty, or revenue share is required solely for that continued
> distribution.
>
> **Surviving Maintenance Rights.** Licensee and authorized maintainers retain
> a perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free right to diagnose, correct, rebuild,
> test, and redistribute each such released End Product using its previously
> licensed Tourmaline version and private engine modifications. Permitted work
> includes bug, crash, security, rendering, accessibility, and platform or
> compatibility fixes. Licensee may apply subsequently published maintenance
> patches applicable to that version, and may backport identifiable corrections
> from newer releases to the extent Licensor controls the relevant rights,
> without licensing unrelated new features. These rights do not authorize new
> End Products, substantial feature development, or general adoption of newer
> feature-bearing engine releases.
>
> **Private Modifications.** Licensee is not required to publish or provide
> its private engine modifications, proprietary extensions, independently
> authored game code, or other original project content. Licensor receives no
> ownership or automatic license to those private modifications merely through
> Licensee's use of Tourmaline.
>
> **No Forced Delisting or Renewal.** Licensor shall not seek removal or
> suspension of an End Product solely due to expiration or nonrenewal of the
> Commercial License. The surviving rights above remain subject to compliance
> with continuing confidentiality, applicable notices, third-party licenses,
> and other express survival obligations of the signed agreement.
>
> **Limits on Maintenance Supply.** Maintenance patches actually published
> by Licensor for eligible covered versions may be used without additional
> engine license fees. Licensor need not develop fixes indefinitely, maintain
> all historic versions, supply nonpublic source, or provide ongoing support
> or hosting unless separately agreed.

## Independent publishing and contractors

A large publisher handling ordinary distribution or non-controlling financing
for a qualifying independent studio does not automatically make the studio
ineligible for Community development. Work-for-hire, group-controlled studios,
or Tourmaline development performed on the publisher's behalf must be assessed
under the Community License and the relevant Commercial License scope.

A commercial contract can cover named or defined affiliates and authorized
contractors for covered projects. A contractor does not automatically receive
rights for unrelated independent projects.

## Existing MIT versions and third-party software

Versions of Tourmaline already released under MIT remain subject to their MIT
license. No new license can retroactively withdraw the rights granted under
those copies. Third-party components, including vendor SDKs and redistributed
binaries, remain under their respective terms as documented in
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

For an inquiry, contact the Tourmaline project maintainer through the
[GitHub project](https://github.com/striker-samuel/tourmaline). Do not post
confidential financial information in a public issue.
