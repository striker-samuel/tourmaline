## 8. Changes in Eligibility

**Ordinary growth.** If an Organization initially qualifies but its Group Annual Revenue reaches or exceeds USD $10,000,000, it must notify Licensor in writing within 30 calendar days after the relevant annual financial results become available. It may continue otherwise permitted development for 90 calendar days after those results become available solely to transition into a Commercial License or wind down the affected new development. After that period it must not undertake further new Tourmaline-based development without a Commercial License.

**Acquisition or change of Control.** If a Qualifying Organization joins an ineligible Corporate Group, its eligibility is reassessed on the effective date of that transaction. Tourmaline projects already in development at that moment may continue for 90 calendar days solely for transition or orderly wind-down. No such period permits the acquiring group or its other affiliates to adopt Tourmaline for new projects.

Transition rights are unavailable to an Organization that was already ineligible when it first began the affected development. A later decrease below the threshold restores Community eligibility for subsequent development only when the Organization actually satisfies the definition, and does not excuse earlier noncompliance.

The continuing distribution and Maintenance permissions in Sections 9 and 10 are separate from these development transition periods.
